## Remaining usability questions

1. **Savings versus income:** one participant required a prompt; R2-P01 is recorded as independently explaining it but also as having hesitated. Ask the facilitator whether hesitation preceded the independent answer, when it happened, and what was actually observed. Both entries are retained; no conclusion that they contradict or agree is assumed.
2. **Existing versus proposed BNPL:** one returning participant requested clarification; a new participant paused but was recorded as independent. Clearer separation may help, but no solution was tested here.
3. **Purchase-plan entry:** one new iPhone participant needed the option pointed out; two other participants are recorded as independent. Discoverability remains a potential issue, not evidence of universal failure.
4. **Mobile scrolling:** the iPhone participant's follow-up summary reports substantial scrolling between inputs and results. The record contains no timing, scroll-distance measurement or broader phone sample.

## Study and course evidence boundaries

The three sessions used a structured instrument and the workbook records three consenting nondevelopers, including two new and one returning. The existing first-round compilation is separate (`docs/user_testing_feedback_review.md`); it is not merged or overwritten. Project brief `project-a-tech-team.pdf` p.3 requires at least three test users and a structured feedback instrument; rubric `marking-criteria-a-tech-team.pdf` p.4 states the same, along with model effectiveness measured against a baseline. **On the supplied records, the minimum count and structured-instrument elements are documented**; participant independence/consent are workbook statements and the team must check the current official course materials and submission context. Saved CFPB baseline/test evidence separately documents model-versus-baseline evaluation; the retest is not model accuracy evidence.

Still needed for a defensible handoff: verify P01's hesitation against contemporaneous field notes; retain original-language notes and distinguish actual words from paraphrases; record exact wording/timing only where available; and describe the tasks, hints, limitations and usability issues in the team's own later assessment. No audio/video exists in the source. If original notes are unavailable, mark details missing rather than reconstructing them. The original workbook does not supply exact viewport sizes or an independently logged app build/consent trail. Whether instructor repository access, final report, video, signed contributions and current Moodle deadlines are complete cannot be determined from this workbook. These are team checks, not claims of completion or missingness beyond the available evidence.

This is a small descriptive retest with one returning and two new participants, different people and potential familiarity/practice and assistance effects. It is not a controlled pre/post comparison, and does not prove that understanding improved, that all users completed tasks independently, or that findings generalize.
