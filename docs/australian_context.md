# Australian BNPL background evidence

Accessed 5 November 2026 (Australia/Sydney). These sources motivate context and applicability discussion only. They are not Australian microdata, external model validation, fairness validation, lending advice or simulator compliance thresholds.

| Source | Publication/version | Supported point | Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|

The original [RG 281 text](https://download.asic.gov.au/media/2sgcg0y5/rg281-published-8-may-2025.pdf), paragraphs 281.1–281.2, describes the regime effective from 10 June 2025. Current original Act volumes were also inspected, including Schedule 1 sections 13B–13E and sections 133BXA–133BXC. This distinguishes effective rules from the earlier consultation process. No live bill or proposal was represented as in-force law.

Financial vulnerability screening and scenario arithmetic can aid explanation, but this project has not implemented a statutory assessment workflow. CFPB is a US contemporaneous survey; unknown-origin Kaggle country categories do not bridge that domain gap. Real Australian matched microdata and a justified independent protocol would be needed for Australian model validation. No Australian income conversion, default estimate, regulatory risk band or safe borrowing amount was added.
